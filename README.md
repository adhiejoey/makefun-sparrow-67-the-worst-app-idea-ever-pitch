SockMatch is the dating app for your orphaned socks, matching them with
strangers' single socks, then charging a breakup fee when the new pair
inevitably splits up over the dryer — a pitch delivered with total founder
confidence about a problem nobody has ever had.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the one-sentence pitch to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(default `/output`).
